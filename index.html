Background & Biology

Breast cancer involves uncontrolled cell growth in breast tissue. Pathologists examine biopsies under microscopes to differentiate between:

  • Benign: Non-cancerous, uniform, structured cells that stay localized.
  • Malignant: Cancerous, chaotic, crowded, irregular cells that can invade tissue.

Manual diagnosis is vulnerable to human fatigue. Automated AI tools offer powerful potential as a second pair of eyes.

Problem & Hypothesis

Research Question

How does the evaluator type (Google Teachable Machine vs. Custom KNN Model vs. Human Non-Experts) affect classification accuracy on breast cancer biopsy images?

Hypothesis

Google Teachable Machine will achieve the highest accuracy because its deep CNN architecture extracts complex morphological patterns, outperforming both the pixel-distance KNN model and untrained humans.

Variables & Setup

Independent Variable:

Evaluator type (Teachable Machine AI, Custom KNN Model, Human Non-Experts).

Dependent Variable:

Classification accuracy percentage (%) on 20 unseen test images.

Controlled Variables:

Identical 20 test slides, identical 50 training slides, and standardized binary scoring.

Key Analysis & Findings

1. CNN Superiority: Google Teachable Machine achieved 90.0% accuracy because its convolutional neural network scans images layer-by-layer to recognize complex shapes and cell cluster textures.
2. KNN Limitations: The Custom KNN Model scored 80.0%. While effective at distance math, it can get tripped up by background staining brightness variations.
3. Human Fatigue: Untrained human evaluators averaged 65.0%, often relying on surface-level color staining rather than deep cellular morphology.

Formal Lab Report

Background Research Summary

Breast cancer involves uncontrolled cell growth in breast tissue. Under a microscope, benign cells appear structured, uniform, and localized, whereas malignant cells are crowded, chaotic, and invasive. Early detection via biopsy saves lives, but pathologist fatigue creates risks, prompting the exploration of AI image classification.

Hypothesis & Problem

Google Teachable Machine will achieve the highest accuracy (90.0%), followed by the Custom KNN Model (80.0%), and Human Non-Experts (65.0%) due to specialized deep learning feature extraction versus pixel distance math and untrained visual estimation.

Conclusion & Acknowledgements

The hypothesis was fully supported. AI tools demonstrate powerful capabilities to serve as assistive screening tools for pathologists, though clinical supervision remains essential. Special thanks to the BreakHis dataset contributors and volunteer evaluators.
